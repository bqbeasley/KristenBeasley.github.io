Why does this website exist?

My work as a software engineer has become stale. Not only have I come to believe that my field is doing a great harm to the world and to the human psyche, but the creativity that I have known is dying. Artificial intelligence has reached a point where it seems that I am legitimately more productive using it than not using it. If I was free, I would be happy to be less productive because it would mean that my work is still art. Unfortunately my livelihood is dependent on my passion and interest and since my livelihood is not my own, but it is the livelihood of other people in my life who I have promised to love and protect, I must do what I need to do to remain competitive and productive.

I have no economic room to fail, and so I cannot stand on my pride and love and forestake capital interests. Given this situation, I have decided to create an artifact of my love before I leave this field. I believe that art thrives within constraint, which can be seen beautifully with 8-bit CRT-TV displayed games. So I have given myself a set of constraints to build this site:

  • No IDEs or coding support may be used. The allowed editor list is vi, vim and notepad.
  • No libraries or external toolings may be used. Everything, including images, must be hand written by me
  • The allowed technology list: css, html, jss, svg, hand drawn images from paper
  • Do not correct grammatical or spelling mistakes. Do not polish. This rule has no hard line, but should serve as a guiding ethos.
  • No AI.
  • Never redact or change information because you later read it as "cringe". Irony is dead, longlive cringe.

The purpose of these rules is to serve as artistic constraints, but more importantly I hope that these constraints will allow this website to be a pure expression of my interior world without filter or mediation. I hope that this will serve as a time capsule of how a real human grieved and loved, how it experienced the world and itself.
