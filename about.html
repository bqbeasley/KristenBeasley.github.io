Why does this website exist?

My work as a software engineer has become stale. Not only have I come to believe that my field is doing a great harm to the world and to the human psyche, but the creativity that I have known is dying. Artificial intelligence has reached a point where in order to maintain my livelihood I am obliged to used it. If I was free, I would be happy to be less productive because it would mean that my work is still art. Unfortunately I am not free.

Give this, my pride is wounded and my passion has died. I've decided to create a small artifact of sincerity before I learn how to leave this field and move onto new loves. I believe that human art and expression thrives within constraints, so I have given myself some:

  • No IDEs or coding support may be used. The allowed editor list is vi, vim and notepad.
  • No libraries or external toolings may be used except for fonts. Everything, including images, must be hand written by me
  • The allowed technology list: css, html, js, svg, hand drawn images from paper
  • Do not correct grammatical or spelling mistakes. Do not polish. This rule has no hard line, but should serve as a guiding ethos.
  • No AI.
  • Never redact or change information because you later read it as "cringe". Irony is dead, longlive cringe.
