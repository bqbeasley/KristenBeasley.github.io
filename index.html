In this time, capitalistic interests have worked tirelessly to expropriate human authenticity itself. Every piece of art, every book, every conversation, every attempt to fight has been subsumed into a simulacrum of human expression that we are then obligated to allow into our lives. This abominable humunculus of human expression consumes the planet as it consumes us, and we seem powerless in the face of drought and decay to scream without that honest display itself being consumed. Capitalism has reached what we can only hope is its the final frontier, the commodification of human creativity and intellect itself.

It is for me easier to imagine to end of the world.

The winters are drier, the summers are hotter, the bugs are silent

and so am I.

What can I do then? The clear answer is nothing. Can I do nothing because I am small, or because I am consumed? If I am small then would I have ever been able to do anything, and if I am consumed then do I matter?

Do I matter?

Red-winged blackbird

We stopped at a historic Russian outpost for Rest and emptied the trash we had accumulated. The woman who worked the counter knew nothing of the local area, she commuted from the inland for work since none could afford to live where we stood. There was an Eastern Orthodox church, with a small decaying icon of our Lord behind poorly maintained glass. We made our way to the waters cliff and felt the joy and awe of the vast sea. A bench was placed at the cliffs edge, what we called the best bench in the world. Coming back from this wonder, the tall grass and I saw a small bird. Like a raven but too small to be one, and I passively wondered who I was in the company of. As we walked they moved to be away from us and in an instant a flash of vibrant red that vanished. A red wing blackbird.

Eastern Redbud

There is abundance, hidden in plain sight. Arkansas is the land of forgotten abundance and joy. Every spring the streets blush with pink inviting the town to share a table, but most do not remember how to listen. The flower s of the Eastern redbud are snappy and midly sweet, giving off a bright neon pigment that brings me joy when mixed with a small amount of acid. In early fall the pods form and peas grow like lentils. Each tree provides more than one could eat, and yet no one eats. Here as with the Ancient PawPaw we have forgotten who feeds us and who cares for us. Yet that gracious mother never stops providing and never fails to decorates our home so that we may still see beauty.

Today (2026) we find ourselves at the end of the great Holocene. Genesis turned out to be a prophecy and we delighted in the tree of knowledge and evil. How evil is the Antropocene? How many species will become extinct at our hands, how many people will suffer in drought and inescapable heat domes? The land has finally decided to vomit us out.

Death defines the Anthropocene.

Life and Earth God gave us as a gift.

At every turn, dispite pleading, we chose death.

Who are the decendents of Cain?
