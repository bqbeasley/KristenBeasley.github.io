In this time, capitalistic interests have worked tirelessly to expropriate human authenticity itself. Every piece of art, every book, every conversation, every attempt to fight has been subsumed into a simulacrum of human expression that we are then obligated to allow into our lives. This abominable humunculus of human expression consumes the planet as it consumes us, and we seem powerless in the face of drought and decay to scream without that honest display itself being consumed. Capitalism has reached what we can only hope is its the final frontier, the commodification of human creativity and intellect itself.

It is for me easier to imagine to end of the world.

The winters are drier, the summers are hotter, the bugs are silent

and so am I.

What can I do then? The clear answer is nothing. Can I do nothing because I am small, or because I am consumed? If I am small then would I have ever been able to do anything, and if I am consumed then do I matter?

Do I matter?

Today (2026) we find ourselves at the end of the great Holocene. Genesis turned out to be a prophecy and we delighted in the tree of knowledge and evil. How evil is the Antropocene? How many species will become extinct at our hands, how many people will suffer in drought and inescapable heat domes? The land has finally decided to vomit us out.

Death defines the Anthropocene.

Life and Earth God gave us as a gift.

At every turn, dispite pleading, we chose death.

Who are the decendents of Cain?
